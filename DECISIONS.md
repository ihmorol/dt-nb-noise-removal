- 2026-09-28 | **E5 pre-registration: graded noise handling — soft / correct / committee vs hard deletion (`code/e5_strategy.py`)** | The user's goal: a noise-removal strategy that actually improves results, on both the instance and attribute side, with mathematical backing. The evidence (E9f/E9p/E3b) says the damage comes from HARD DELETION of the judge's hard rows, so the strategy family replaces the 0/1 deletion decision with graded handling. Fixed before any run: three judges on the raw training fold — FaithfulNB (the seed's judge), J48 pruned defaults, Weka Logistic defaults (three model families so their errors are less correlated); treatments: (soft) w_i = P_judge(y_i|x_i), no deletion, fractional counts for NB and ARFF instance weights for J48; (correct) relabel to c' iff >=2 of 3 judges predict the SAME c' != y_i, nothing deleted — vote-based, not posterior-threshold-based, because NB posteriors saturate and a confidence threshold would degenerate into distilling the judge; (committee) delete iff >=2 of 3 judges misclassify (Brodley & Friedl's majority partnership); (attr) Algorithm 2's 1/sqrt(min-depth) weights from the raw-fold tree for the NB finals. Arms: {soft, correct, committee} -> DT; {baseline, soft, attr, softattr, correct} -> NB; protocol B only; 10 seeds x stratified 10-fold; hard->DT and the baselines come from E9f (identical folds and engines, bit-comparable). Mathematical backing: deletion removes exactly the boundary mass whose labels are usually correct (its damage tracked the deletion fraction in E9p: glass 47% deleted -> -17; iris 4% -> ~0); posterior weighting is the plug-in of the label-quality posterior P(correct|x,y), the single-model case of importance reweighting for label noise (Liu & Tao 2015) and confident learning's self-confidence (Northcutt et al. 2021); majority voting suppresses false deletions quadratically under judge-error independence (Condorcet: 3e^2-2e^3 << e; measured by Brodley & Friedl 1999); correction keeps the row's real feature information and replaces a label that, under cross-family agreement, is more probably wrong than right (Jiang et al. 2024: correction beats filtering; ledger L31). Success criteria fixed in advance: PRIMARY = soft->DT vs hard->DT paired over the same folds; SECONDARY = each arm vs the no-cleaning baselines. Beating hard deletion is what the theory predicts; beating the no-cleaning baseline ON AVERAGE on these mostly-clean datasets is promised by no theory and is not the success criterion — where it happens (real label noise) it is a per-dataset finding | Sweeping tau thresholds on NB posteriors (saturated, uninterpretable, would be tuning to the answer); relabeling every judge error (distills the weaker judge into the stronger); any protocol-A runs (the leakage lesson is established); tuning the soft weight power or floor (one pre-registered form only)

- 2026-10-02 | **E12 configuration (pre-registered BEFORE any E12 result was seen): ground-truth label-noise injection + per-class confident-joint filtering, DT and NB judged SEPARATELY** | (1) **Inject known noise.** E11 could not answer "is this filter good?" because all ten UCI datasets are clean, so there is no ground truth to rank filters by - every E11 delta landed inside p>=0.0625 for that reason, not because the arms were equivalent. `noise_inject.py` corrupts a controlled fraction of TRAINING-FOLD labels only (symmetric and asymmetric/class-dependent; the confusable-class map is estimated by cross-validated NB inside the same fold) and keeps the corruption mask, so each filter is graded on precision/recall/AUROC against truth.
